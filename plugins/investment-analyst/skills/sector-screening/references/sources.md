# Screening sources and cross-checks

The toolkit pulls Yahoo Finance (via yfinance) automatically. Use these sites to **cross-check** important
names by hand or to explore. They are not scraped.

| Site | Use it for | URL |
|---|---|---|
| Finviz | Fast free first-pass filters (valuation, cap, performance), industry lists | https://finviz.com/screener.ashx |
| Koyfin | Clean peer comparisons, estimate history, valuation history charts | https://www.koyfin.com |
| Stock Analysis | Free financials, multiples, historical valuation | https://stockanalysis.com |
| Zacks | Estimate-revision trends and earnings-surprise history | https://www.zacks.com |
| SEC EDGAR full-text search | Find a phrase across filings (e.g. "material weakness") | https://efts.sec.gov/LATEST/search-index?q= or https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/ |
| Company investor relations | Earnings decks, guidance, investor-day targets | company website |
| FDA calendars (health care) | Approval/PDUFA dates | https://www.fda.gov |

When a cross-check disagrees with the toolkit by a lot (e.g. forward P/E 14 vs 22), say so, prefer the
source closest to the filings, and flag the name for verification in the deep dive.
